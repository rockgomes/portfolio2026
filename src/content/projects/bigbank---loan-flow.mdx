<CaseSection title="How I got there" subtitle="the process, short" id="process">
  <CaseBlock label="research" title="Enough data, as fast as possible" pills={['clients', 'country managers', 'Legal', 'Accounting']}>
    A bank sells money. It must follow regulations and do risk checks, so it needs a lot of data. Each country has its own laws and required checks.

    Clients want what the money pays for: a new TV, a broken appliance, a family trip. They have to wait for the bank before they can get it, so they want a fast yes or no. At that time, many loans took days to answer.

    We designed each step to collect enough information for the bank to start its decision early. We kept the flow as clear and short as possible. Lawyers, accountants and other teams checked that every question was needed and that none was missing.
  </CaseBlock>
  <CaseBlock label="scope" title="One design for every country">
    Before, each country made its own decisions about its flow. Now 5 countries share one design. We did Finland, Estonia and Sweden together, then I did Latvia and Lithuania alone. Everything is responsive and mobile-first.
  </CaseBlock>
</CaseSection>
