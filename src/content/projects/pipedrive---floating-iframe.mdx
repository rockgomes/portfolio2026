<CaseSection title="Two decisions I would defend" subtitle="where to start, and what to build" id="decisions">
  <CaseBlock label="decision 1 of 2" data-decision="1" title="Start with calling apps" pills={['used a lot', 'no good way to sync call data']}>
    The data showed that people use calling integrations a lot, and these integrations had no good way to sync call data. We looked at a few kinds of integrations to start with. Calling apps were the best first test of the idea. Also, Pipedrive had just dropped its plan for its own calling feature, so we wanted to make the calling integrations better.
  </CaseBlock>
  <CaseBlock label="decision 2 of 2" data-decision="2" title="A floating window that users can move or minimise">
    Users wanted quick access to the integration that is always there, and also a way to hide it. They wanted alerts. They also wanted links from the app into Pipedrive, for example to open the page of the person who is calling. So we put the app in a floating window. Users can move it anywhere on the page or minimise it.

<CaseSection title="How I got there" subtitle="the process, short" id="process">
  <CaseBlock label="interviews" title="Talk to partners and users" pills={['4 or 5 partner calls', '10 to 12 user interviews']}>
    With partners, my team and I talked about the data they could share, pricing models and their experience with other CRMs. With users, I guided the conversation so they answered my questions without me asking them directly.
  </CaseBlock>
  <CaseBlock label="design rounds" title="Work with the whole design team">
    The idea changed the core product, so the whole design team had to agree on it. I shared drafts with engineers, the product manager and other designers all the time. It took many design rounds.
  </CaseBlock>
  <CaseBlock label="testing" title="Four prototypes, two per interview" pills={['4 prototypes', '2 per interview', 'order changed each time']}>
    Each prototype tested a different idea, for example a different entry point. In each interview, users did the same task in two prototypes and compared them. The order changed in every interview.
