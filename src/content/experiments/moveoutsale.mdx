<CaseSection title="How it is built" subtitle="the part that was interesting" id="built">
  <CaseBlock label="the idea" title="One moving sale, not fourteen posts">
    A moving sale holds everything one person sells before they leave. Buyers see the whole clear-out in one place.
    <Proof slot="proof" caption="One moving sale: a flat clear-out in Kalamaja, six items." media={img(sale, 'The Kalamaja flat clear-out page in MoveOutSale: one seller, Marta Kuusk from Tallinn, with six items in a grid, among them a grey sofa, an oak dining table, a brass lamp and a cast iron pan.')} />
  </CaseBlock>
  <CaseBlock label="the interesting part" title="Clickable in public, without the real data">
    The demo runs on its own Supabase project and never holds the production keys. Every visitor is signed in as their own throwaway account. Row-level security keeps visitors apart, and a noindex header keeps what they type out of search results.
  </CaseBlock>
  <CaseBlock label="a small thing" title="Reset only when something changed">
    A scheduled job wipes and re-seeds the demo every night, but only if someone changed something. Most nights it checks, finds nothing, and does no work.
  </CaseBlock>
  <CaseBlock label="the stack" title="The rules live in the database" pills={['Next.js', 'Supabase', 'Playwright']}>
    Next.js App Router, with Supabase for Postgres, sign-in and file storage. Every table sits behind row-level security. Photos are made smaller in the browser before upload. Playwright covers the main flows.
  </CaseBlock>
</CaseSection>
