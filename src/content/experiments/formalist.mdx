<CaseSection title="How it is built" subtitle="the part that was interesting" id="built">
  <CaseBlock label="the idea" title="Save it once, fill any form">
    Formalist finds the fields it can fill and puts a Fill badge in each one. Fill them all at once, or pick a value for one field. Undo puts everything back.
    <ProofStrip slot="proof" phones caption="The Formalist window: the fields it found on this page, then the same list after one click." items={[
      { src: popupDetected, label: 'form found', alt: 'The Formalist window: Form detected, 9 of 9 fields matched, a Fill 9 fields button, and the list of detected fields with Alex Taylor’s saved answers.' },
      { src: popupFilled, label: 'after Fill 9 fields', alt: 'The Formalist window after the fill: Filled 9 fields with an Undo button, and the line Nothing is submitted. You still press the form’s own button.' },
    ]} />
  </CaseBlock>
  <CaseBlock label="the hard part" title="The badge stays next to its field">
    Job sites often show the form in a pop-up window or inside a frame. I made sure the badge stays next to its field there too, and that a click on it does not close the window. I tested on LinkedIn, Greenhouse, InvestEngine and Teamtailor.
    <BadgeLayers slot="proof" caption="Sites I tested on: LinkedIn Easy Apply, forms inside frames, Greenhouse phone fields, InvestEngine and Teamtailor." />
  </CaseBlock>
  <CaseBlock label="security" title="Checked before launch">
    Before launch I ran a security audit and fixed what I found.
  </CaseBlock>
  <CaseBlock label="how it was made" title="Built with AI, reviewed like a team">
    I designed in Figma and built with Claude Code. Every change, more than 50 so far, went through a branch, a pull request, tests in CI, and my review.
